# Opportunity Finder (Beginner-Friendly)

This repository now contains a simple offline tool that helps you choose the best practical opportunity and take action.

## Why this is useful

If you're a beginner, choosing what to do is usually the hardest part. This tool helps you:
- compare multiple ideas using clear criteria,
- rank them objectively,
- pick one best next action,
- and keep your decisions saved in your browser.

## How to use (no coding needed)

1. Open `/home/runner/work/Intelligence-/Intelligence-/index.html` in your browser.
2. Fill one opportunity at a time.
3. Use 1-10 scores:
   - Higher is better for: value, feasibility, repeatability
   - Lower is better for: cost, time, risk
4. Add one tiny real-world experiment for each idea.
5. Click **Add and score**.
6. Start with the top-ranked item shown under **Best next action**.

## Files

- `index.html` - the app UI
- `styles.css` - styling
- `app.js` - scoring and ranking logic

## Notes

- Your entries are auto-saved locally in your browser.
- **Export JSON** downloads your opportunity list for backup.
- **Clear all** removes saved entries from this browser.
